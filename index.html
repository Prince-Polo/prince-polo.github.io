2026arXiv preprint

Parallelism, critical windows, and separations among diffusion language models

Sitan Chen and Liye Wang

For a family of distributions on sequences of length dd, uniform and Gaussian diffusion take roughly d\sqrt{d} forward passes, while masked diffusion can need nearly dd with an approximate score oracle. The separation comes from the narrower range of noise levels at which information about the sample emerges under masked diffusion.
